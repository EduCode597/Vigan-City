Why Vigan holds a singular place in the Philippines

While most Spanish-era urban centers in the archipelago were flattened during World War II, Vigan survived intact. Its cobblestone grid fuses Ilocano timber craft, Chinese merchant stonework, and Spanish plaza planning into a living town you can still walk end to end.
